# Founder video concepts: 20 hooks that lead to branditchina.com

Formats are written for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and
LinkedIn video. Each one opens on the hook within the first second, uses
the opening graphic from `brand/social` for no more than half a second,
and closes on the closing frame with the domain. Every concept is filmed
on location in China or Asia with the founder on camera. Nothing in these
scripts should make a claim about certifications, approvals or results
that cannot be shown or evidenced.

Recurring caption treatment: white Plus Jakarta Sans, left aligned, on a
charcoal bar with the vermilion rule above. Lower third: founder name, "Founder, Brand It
China", wordmark (`brand/social/lower-third-16x9.png`).

## Hook library (rotate across platforms)

- "I'm in a factory in China right now and..."
- "The thing nobody tells you about private label..."
- "Here's the sample. Here's what the factory said. Here's what I said."
- "You could own this brand. Not buy it. Own it."
- "Stop searching directories. Start with a conversation."

## Series structure

- Monday: on location (1, 2, 9, 17, 18)
- Wednesday: education (3, 5, 7, 8, 13, 14, 16)
- Friday: product (4, 10, 11, 12, 19, 20)

Every video ends on the closing frame. The link in bio is always
branditchina.com, and the pinned comment repeats the WhatsApp call to
action.
