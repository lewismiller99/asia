BRAND IT CHINA | APPROVED BRAND PACK | V1.0

Start with Brand_It_China_Brand_Guidelines.pdf.
Approved logo: stacked lowercase brand it / china, long t and red Chinese create seal. No slogan or Chinese descriptor underneath.

SVG, PDF and EPS logos contain vector outlines, not embedded bitmap images. The original artwork has been traced and cleaned from the approved concept with minor curve tolerances. Use this artwork rather than retyping the logo.

Primary: charcoal wordmark and red seal, for light backgrounds.
Reverse: white wordmark and red seal, for dark backgrounds.
Mono_Black and Mono_White: single ink artwork. The contrasting seal character is a knockout in these variants.
Wordmark_Black and Wordmark_White: secondary artwork without seal for limited production uses.
Seal_Red, Seal_Black and Seal_White: standalone seals for secondary brand details.
All PNGs have transparent backgrounds. White artwork needs a dark background to be visible.

Core colours: Charcoal #202020; Vermilion #E63329; White #FFFFFF.
Supporting colours: Light grey #E6E6E6; Mid grey #737373.
CMYK values in the guide and JSON are approximate conversions. Approve physical print proofs.

Supporting fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold 800 for headings; Inter Regular 400 for body and SemiBold 600 for labels. Original variable font files and licences are included. These are supporting fonts, not an exact identification of the custom logo lettering.
Font sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/plusjakartasans and https://github.com/rsms/inter

The Chinese seal contains the simplified character meaning create, pronounced chuang (fourth tone). It is supplied as artwork and needs no installed font.
Keep at least one seal width clear around the logo. Start at 220 px or 55 mm total width and check legibility at actual size. Supplier specific formats such as DST embroidery require separate digitising and a production sample.
